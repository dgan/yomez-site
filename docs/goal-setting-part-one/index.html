Become more effective: a self-guided goal setting workshop

July 17, 2020 Productivity

When setting out to be productive, you attempt to do one of two things. Either to :

Be efficient – get things done

or

Be effective – get the right things done

At Yomez, we believe in getting the right things done—or being effective. In other words, focusing one’s time and energy on not only getting things done but getting things done that drive results and help you accomplish your goals or objectives. So, what are your goals? 

It’s no secret that goal setting is challenging. For this reason, we created a self-guided workshop to help you figure out what you value, what you want, and how to become the most effective version of yourself.

    • Part One – Getting to know yourself
    • Part Two – Creating your personal mission statement
    • Part Three – Determining your destination

Each week, for three weeks, we’ll send a worksheet straight to your inbox to guide you on your journey—just sign up below to start. By the time you complete the self-guided workshop, you’ll have goals aligned with your values, principles, and vision.

    What you’ll get out of the self-guided workshop

    In Part One: Getting to know yourself, you’ll learn about:

      • Your centers. What you’ve placed at the center of your life and if it’s serving you.
      • New principles. What principles can help you become the most effective version of yourself.
      • Existing & new values. What you value—or didn’t know you valued—and how to use them to inform where you’re going and who you’ll become.

    With Part Two – Creating your personal mission statement, you’ll:

      • Learn what personal mission statements are, and why they matter
      • Explore examples of personal mission statements from some of the most successful people in society
      • Reflect on personal talents, influences, and values
      • Develop your own personal mission statement

    Conclude the self-guided workshop with Part Three – Determining your destination. In this section you’ll:

      • Develop long-term goals. Identify and shape your long-term visit by incorporating your talents, values, and principles
      • Establish smaller short-term goals. Increase your likelihood of reaching your long-term goals by focusing on specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and timely short-term goals

    Ready to be effective and establish long-term goals for yourself? Start today. The worst that can happen is that you learn a little more about yourself.
